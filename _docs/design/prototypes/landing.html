The four-act structure

01Scene

Nominate

Each friend picks one film. Pull from TMDB's catalog or reuse your bench of past picks that didn't win. One nomination per round, no exceptions — the director's cut.

02Scene

Select

Score each nomination. Live progress keeps everyone in the loop. Admins close the vote when ready — deterministic tiebreaker rules ensure a winner without arguments.

03Scene

Watch

Log the session — date, location, notes. The winner is recorded in your club's shared history. Every session builds the canon.

04Scene

Rate

Rate the film 1–10 afterward. Build a leaderboard that reflects your club's real taste over time — not an algorithm's guess, but your crew's actual verdict.
