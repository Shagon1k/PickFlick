Cancel subscription

Cancelling your Pro subscription will take effect at the end of the current billing period. Existing groups and members above the Free limit are not removed — you just can't create new ones or invite new members until you're under the limit.

PickFlick stores your profile (display name, avatar, email), club memberships, session nominations, votes, movie ratings, and session history. We do not collect location data, browsing history outside PickFlick, or any third-party tracking data.

Your ratings and nominations are used to power club leaderboards, session recommendations, and your personal watch history. Club admins can see your nominations and ratings within their club. Your email is only used for account recovery, notifications you opt into, and service announcements.

We never sell your data. We do not share your personal information with third parties except as required by law. Anonymous, aggregated club statistics (e.g. "average rating across all clubs") may be used for product insights.

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days. Inactive clubs are automatically archived after 180 days of no activity.

You can export your data at any time in CSV format. You can request a full GDPR-compliant archive of all your data. You can delete your account permanently — this removes all your data, club memberships, and cancels any active subscriptions.

PickFlick uses only essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies. No cookie consent banner is required.

Danger zone

Deleting your account is permanent. All your clubs (where you are admin), memberships, nominations, votes, ratings, and session history will be removed. Active subscriptions will be cancelled.
